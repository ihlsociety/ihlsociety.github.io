Paper Submission

Submission guidelines, publication tracks, and author requirements

General Requirements

Authors are invited to submit original papers to one of the two tracks. Please ensure that you select the correct track for your submission. The same paper must not be submitted to both tracks.

  • Submissions must be original.
  • Submissions must not be under review by another journal or conference.
  • All submissions will undergo peer review by the International Programme Committee.

Track 1: International Track

  • Publication: Springer CCIS series
  • Indexing: Scopus, EI Compendex
  • Language: English only
  • Format: Springer CCIS template (Word or LaTeX)
  • Paper length: up to 12 pages including references
  • Submission Portal: Microsoft CMT

Track 2: Vocational Education Track

  • Publication: Local Conference Proceedings
  • Language: Chinese only
  • Topic Restriction: Must relate to vocational and professional education and training
  • Format: Local Proceedings Template
  • Submission Portal: Microsoft CMT
  • More details will be revealed later

Submission Process

1

Choose a Track

Select either the International Track (Track 1) or the Vocational Education Track (Track 2) based on your language and topic.

2

Prepare Your Manuscript

Format your manuscript using the appropriate template and ensure it satisfies the requirements.

3

Submit via Microsoft CMT

Upload your manuscript to the correct submission track on the designated conference portal. Links to the portal will be revealed later.

4

Peer Review

The International Programme Committee will review all submissions.

5

Camera-Ready & Registration

Accepted papers must complete camera-ready submission and at least one full registration.

6

In-Person Presentation

Each accepted paper must be presented in person at the conference venue. Track 1 presentations are in English; Track 2 presentations are in Chinese.
