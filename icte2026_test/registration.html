Registration

Registration rates and participation policy for ICTE 2026

Registration Policy

  • Each accepted paper must have at least one full registration to be included in the conference proceedings.
  • Each accepted paper must be presented in person at the conference venue.
  • Track 1 papers must be presented in English.
  • Track 2 papers must be presented in Chinese.
  • Failure to present may result in removal from the final publication.
  • Only electronic proceedings (e-proceedings) will be provided; no printed proceedings will be issued.
  • In accordance with international conference practice and financial regulations, the registration fee is non-refundable if you are unable to attend due to personal reasons.
