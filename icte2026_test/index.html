Reshaping Education with Intelligent Technologies

Welcome to the official website of the 9th International Conference on Technology in Education (ICTE 2026), a premier platform for academic exchange on innovative practices and empirical studies in educational technology. ICTE 2026 will be held from 2 to 4 December 2026 in Yangzhou, China.

Paper submission Registration

About ICTE 2026

Technology has become an integral force in virtually all aspects of education, broadly covering curriculum planning, content development and delivery, communication among learners, instructors, and institutions, as well as assessment and programme evaluation.

Following the success of previous conferences, ICTE 2026 aims to serve as a premier platform for relevant academic exchanges. Under the theme “Reshaping Education with Intelligent Technologies”, the conference will focus on sharing good practices, innovative methodologies, and empirical findings that demonstrate how emerging technologies are transforming education.

We warmly welcome administrators, teaching staff, researchers, graduate students, and industry professionals involved in educational technology to participate.

Call for Papers

ICTE 2026 invites original papers across two publication tracks.

Track 1: International Track

Accepted papers will be published in Springer's Communications in Computer and Information Science (CCIS) series (subject to confirmation), indexed by major academic databases including Scopus and EI Compendex.

  • Language: English
  • Format: Springer CCIS template
  • Paper length: up to 12 pages including references
  • Submission portal: Microsoft CMT

Track 2: Vocational Education Track

Accepted papers will be published in the Local Conference Proceedings. This track is dedicated to research and practices related to vocational and professional education. More details will be revealed later.

  • Language: Chinese
  • Topic focus: Vocational and professional education and training
  • Format: Local Proceedings Template

Important Notes

  • Submissions must be original and not under review elsewhere.
  • All papers will undergo peer review by the International Programme Committee.
  • Authors must select the correct track before submission.
  • The same paper must not be submitted to both tracks.
