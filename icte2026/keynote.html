Education 4.0: Higher Education Transformation in the Era of AI

Abstract: Higher education is entering a period of rapid disruption driven by artificial intelligence, digital technologies, changing workforce needs and evolving learner expectations. Universities must therefore adapt their education model to remain relevant, competitive and impactful. In this paper, we propose Education 4.0 (E4.0) as a strategic response to this changing landscape, advancing a paradigm shift towards student-centred, personalised and competency-based education. We outline its educational foundations and five institutional objectives, and present the SCALE framework to coordinate curriculum redesign, AI capability development, AI-enabled education platforms, linked data, systems and processes, and evidence-based measurement and governance. Then, we translate this vision into actions across curriculum, people, learning support, services and collaboration. We further present how The Hong Kong Polytechnic University is advancing E4.0 through strategic initiatives and educational practices that connect curriculum transformation with institutional support. These efforts provide a reference for universities pursuing student-centred, personalised and competency-based education in the AI era.

From Design to Reality: Critical Issues in Intelligent Technologies for Disengaged Urban Students

Abstract: Urban education faces persistent systemic challenges, including poverty, migration, violence, and high dropout rates, that worsen when schools fail to meet student needs. Drawing from a decade-long partnership between two upper secondary schools and a foreign business community, this ex-ploratory study identifies and describes critical issues when developing AI chatbots for disengaged learners in two urban schools, located in Bang-kok. While chatbots are widely used for commercial purpose, vulnerable educational settings require a human-centric, empathetic framework to avoid the gap between conceptual design and operational realities. Ana-lyzing the results from semi-structured interviews with teachers and stu-dents through Interpretative Phenomenological Analysis, this study re-veals that integrating student voices prior to technical deployment is vital for sustaining its utilization. The study outlines the necessity of balancing academic and personal responses, aligning chatbot language with student familiarity, and establishing strict ethical protocols to manage low- and high-risk student queries. Finally, the limitations of this study are dis-cussed, and directions for further exploratory studies are proposed.

Research on Teachers' Artificial Intelligence Literacy in the Intelligent Era

Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI) is reshaping teachers’ roles and responsibilities. This study reviews the development of AI literacy and analyzes seven representative international frameworks. It identifies five shared principles: human-centered values and teacher agency; AI understanding and critical judgment; educational purpose and contextual fit; human-AI collaboration and output verification; and ethics, safety, and responsible practice. Based on this synthesis, the study proposes a five‑dimensional framework for teachers’ AI literacy: AI awareness and mindset, AI knowledge and skills, AI application in education, AI ethics and safety, and AI-supported professional development. This framework provides a conceptual reference for teacher training, assessment, and continued professional development.

The Research on Instructional Design for Future Classrooms in Vocational Education Empowered by Artificial Intelligence

Abstract: With the advancement of the National Education Digitalization Strategy Action Plan 2.0 and "AI + Vocational Education", leveraging the classroom as the primary venue for cultivating talent and utilizing artificial intelligence to construct future classrooms in vocational education has become a critical breakthrough. This approach addresses three major challenges facing vocational education—personalization, high quality, and scale—and serves to deepen teaching reform while fostering high-quality technical and skilled personnel.

Based on a systematic review of the current status and trends in AI-empowered pedagogical reform in vocational education, as well as the progress and existing issues in teacher-student-machine triadic synergy within future classrooms, this study is guided by theories of human-AI collaborative education, instructional design, and scenario-based teaching. Integrating the specific characteristics of vocational education, the research first clarifies the connotation, features, and construction methods of future classrooms in this context. It then explores instructional design methodologies for AI-empowered future classrooms in vocational education, proposing core design concepts, content frameworks, and procedural models. Finally, the study investigates instructional design patterns for these classrooms, establishing models such as personalized learning and skills training, project-driven and case-based teaching, and virtual-real blended simulation training. Additionally, it provides practical pathways for implementing instructional design in AI-empowered future vocational classrooms. This research aims to offer theoretical support and practical references for constructing AI-empowered future classrooms in vocational education.
