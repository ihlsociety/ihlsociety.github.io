Discover Yangzhou

A timeless city where classical gardens meet ancient canals. Home to UNESCO World Heritage Sites, millennia-old temples, and one of China's most celebrated culinary traditions—Yangzhou promises an unforgettable backdrop for ICTE 2026.

Explore attractions Getting here

A City of Culture and Canals

Yangzhou (扬州), located at the confluence of the Yangtze River and the Grand Canal in central Jiangsu Province, is a city steeped in over 2,500 years of history. Once a thriving hub during the Tang and Qing dynasties, its prosperity was built on salt trade and canal commerce, attracting poets, painters, and scholars for centuries.

Today, Yangzhou is a harmonious blend of heritage and modernity. Its meticulously preserved gardens, tranquil lakes, and vibrant historic streets co-exist with a dynamic, innovation-driven economy. Recognised as a National Historical and Cultural City of China, Yangzhou was awarded the UN Habitat Scroll of Honour for its sustainable urban development in 2006.

From the serenity of Slender West Lake at dawn to the bustling flavours of Dongguan Street at dusk, every corner of Yangzhou tells a story. We warmly invite ICTE 2026 attendees to experience this cultural gem.

Iconic Yangzhou Experiences

From UNESCO-listed gardens to timeless canal-side streets—your curated guide to Yangzhou.

Slender West Lake

A masterpiece of Chinese landscape design and a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Stroll along willow-lined embankments, cross the iconic Five Pavilion Bridge (五亭桥), and admire the White Dagoba reflected in still waters.

Photo: Gisling / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Geyuan Garden (个园)

One of China's Four Famous Gardens, Geyuan is renowned for its bamboo groves and four-season rockery. Each rock formation evokes a different season—spring, summer, autumn, and winter—making it a poetic walk through the year.

Photo: rheins / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

Daming Temple (大明寺)

Perched on Shugang Hill, this ancient Buddhist temple dates back to the 5th century. The towering Qiling Pagoda offers panoramic views of Yangzhou. It was here that the monk Jianzhen (鉴真) studied before his legendary voyages to Japan.

Photo: Gisling / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

Dongguan Street (东关街)

Step back in time on this 1,200-metre-long historic pedestrian street lined with Ming and Qing dynasty architecture. Sample local delicacies, browse artisan crafts, and soak up the vibrant street life of old Yangzhou.

Photo: rheins / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

Grand Canal (京杭大运河)

The world's longest artificial waterway, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. The Yangzhou section of the Grand Canal was the city's economic lifeline for centuries. Take an evening cruise to see Yangzhou's lights dance on the water.

Photo: jaysonh / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

He Garden (何园)

Also known as Jixiao Mountain Villa, He Garden is a late-Qing masterpiece blending Chinese and Western architectural styles. Its winding covered corridors and elegant rockeries make it a photographer's delight.

Photo: Gisling / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 3.0

Getting to Yangzhou

Yangzhou is well-connected by air, high-speed rail, and expressway. Here are the most convenient routes for international and domestic travellers.

✈ By Air

Yangzhou Taizhou International Airport (YTY) serves domestic routes to major Chinese cities and select international destinations. The airport is approximately 40 km from the city centre.

Alternative airports within reach:

  • Nanjing Lukou International Airport (NKG) – ~100 km, connected by high-speed rail (~40 min)
  • Shanghai Pudong International Airport (PVG) – connected by high-speed rail (~2.5 hrs)

🚄 By High-Speed Rail

Yangzhou East Railway Station (扬州东站) connects the city to China's extensive high-speed rail network:

  • Nanjing → Yangzhou: ~40 minutes
  • Shanghai → Yangzhou: ~2 hours
  • Beijing → Yangzhou: ~4.5 hours
  • Hangzhou → Yangzhou: ~2.5 hours

🚗 Local Transport

Getting around Yangzhou is convenient and affordable:

  • Taxis & Ride-hailing: Didi Chuxing is widely available
  • Public Buses: Extensive network covering all major attractions
  • Public Bicycles: Dockless bike-sharing services throughout the city
  • City Shuttle: Complimentary shuttle bus service will be arranged for ICTE 2026 delegates between recommended hotels and the conference venue

December in Yangzhou

What to expect and how to prepare for your visit during the conference period.

🌤 Weather

Early December in Yangzhou is crisp and cool, with average daytime temperatures ranging from 2°C to 10°C (36°F–50°F). Occasional light rain is possible. We recommend bringing warm layers, a coat, and comfortable walking shoes.

🍜 Local Cuisine

Yangzhou is famed for Huaiyang cuisine (淮扬菜), one of China's Four Great Culinary Traditions. Must-try dishes include Yangzhou fried rice (扬州炒饭), lion's head meatballs (狮子头), and delicate steamed soup dumplings. The city's teahouse culture is not to be missed.

💰 Practical Info

  • Currency: Chinese Yuan (CNY / RMB)
  • Language: Mandarin Chinese (English signage is common in tourist areas)
  • Power: 220V, 50Hz – Type A, C, and I plugs
  • Payment: WeChat Pay and Alipay are ubiquitous; international credit cards are accepted at major hotels
  • Time Zone: China Standard Time (UTC+8)
