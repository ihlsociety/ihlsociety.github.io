Author Guidelines

Paper submissions for both tracks closed on 7 September 2026.

General Requirements

Each paper could be submitted to only one of the two tracks.

  • Submissions must be original.
  • Submissions must not be under review by another journal or conference.
  • The International Programme Committee oversees peer review for both tracks.

Track 1: International Track

  • Publication: Springer CCIS proceedings volume
  • Indexing: Scopus, EI Compendex
  • Language: English only
  • Format: Springer CCIS template (Word or LaTeX format)
  • Paper length: up to 12 pages including references
  • Submission Deadline: 17 July 2026 AOE 7 September 2026 (final)

Track 2: Vocational Education Track

  • Publication: Local Conference Proceedings
  • Language: Chinese only
  • Topic Restriction: Must relate to vocational and professional education and training
  • Format: Local Proceedings Template
  • Paper length: up to 10 pages including references
  • Submission Deadline: 17 July 2026 AOE 7 September 2026 (final)

IEEE Transactions on Learning Technologies (TLT) Recommendation

Selected papers from Track 1: International Track will be invited to submit significantly extended versions to this JCR Q1, SSCI-SCI-EI indexed journal for publishing. Please refer to TLT author center on the requirements for extended conference papers.

Submission Process

1

Track Selection

Authors chose either the International Track (Track 1) or the Vocational Education Track (Track 2) based on their paper’s language and topic.

2

Manuscript Preparation

Each track has its own manuscript template and formatting requirements.

3

Paper Submission

Submissions closed on 7 September 2026.

CMT Acknowledgement: The Microsoft CMT service was used for managing the peer-reviewing process for this conference. This service was provided for free by Microsoft and they bore all expenses, including costs for Azure cloud services as well as for software development and support.

4

Peer Review

The International Programme Committee manages the review of submitted papers.

5

Camera-Ready & Registration

Accepted papers must complete camera-ready submission and at least one full registration.

6

In-Person Presentation

Each accepted paper must be presented in person at the conference venue. Track 1 presentations are in English; Track 2 presentations are in Chinese.
